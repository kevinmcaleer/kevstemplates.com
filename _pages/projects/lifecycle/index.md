# Project Lifecycle

Every project, whatever its size or method, passes through five stages: it is started up, initiated, planned, delivered and closed. Small projects move through some stages in days; large ones run planning and delivery as several stages, each ending with a board decision. Click a stage for its guide, or a template to open it.
