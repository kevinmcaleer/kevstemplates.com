---

## Why bother

**It's where the retro comes from.** At day 90 nobody remembers week 3. The log does — and honest contemporaneous notes beat reconstructed memory every time.

**It catches drift early.** Three weeks of "blocked on the same decision" is visible in a way that three separate conversations aren't.

**"What I was wrong about" builds the right habit.** It keeps you diagnosing rather than defending, and it's the discipline that separates people who get better at this from people who accumulate experience without learning.

**It's your evidence.** When someone says "we never raised that", the log has the date.
