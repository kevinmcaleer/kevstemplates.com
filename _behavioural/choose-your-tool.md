A decision tree for when you don't know where to start.

---

### "People aren't using the new system."

Do you know **why** yet — from talking to them, not from a survey?

- **No** → [Barrier Analysis — COM-B Interview Guide](/behavioural-change/barrier-analysis-com-b-interview-guide/). Six conversations will tell you more than a 400-response survey.
- **Yes, they can't** (skill, tools, access) → Capability/Opportunity problem → [Intervention Design Worksheet](/behavioural-change/intervention-design-worksheet/), look at Training and Enablement.
- **Yes, they won't** (don't see the point, prefer the old way) → Motivation problem → [Social Norms and Influence](/behavioural-change/social-norms-and-influence/) + [Resistance Decoder](/behavioural-change/resistance-decoder/).
- **Yes, they forget** → habit problem → [Habit Loop and Behavioural Drift](/behavioural-change/habit-loop-and-behavioural-drift/) + [Nudge Library](/behavioural-change/nudge-library/) (prompts and defaults).

⚠️ The single most common error: assuming "won't" when it's "can't". Managers reliably over-attribute to attitude. See [Myths and Traps in Change Management](/behavioural-change/myths-and-traps-in-change-management/).

---

### "We need a change plan by Friday."

[Change on a Page](/behavioural-change/change-on-a-page/) → fill it in → [The One-Page Board Narrative](/behavioural-change/the-one-page-board-narrative/) for the exec version.

If you have 90 minutes with the right people first, run [Workshop — Behavioural Diagnosis (90 min)](/behavioural-change/workshop-behavioural-diagnosis-90-min/) and the plan writes itself.

---

### "The sponsor isn't showing up."

[Sponsor Briefing Pack](/behavioural-change/sponsor-briefing-pack/) to make the ask concrete → [Leader Behaviour Contract](/behavioural-change/leader-behaviour-contract/) to make it visible → [Challenging Upward — Scripts](/behavioural-change/challenging-upward-scripts/) if it continues.

Active, visible sponsorship is repeatedly identified as the largest single contributor to change success. A quiet sponsor is not a minor risk — it is *the* risk.

---

### "Leadership say the right things but do the opposite."

[Executive Failure Modes](/behavioural-change/executive-failure-modes/) (specifically *Say–Do Gap*) → [Leader Behaviour Contract](/behavioural-change/leader-behaviour-contract/).

The organisation reads behaviour, not memos. One leader visibly bypassing the new process undoes six weeks of comms.

---

### "There's a lot of resistance."

[Resistance Decoder](/behavioural-change/resistance-decoder/) first. Resistance is almost always one of: unclear ask, real cost you haven't acknowledged, loss of status/competence, prior broken promises, or a genuinely bad design. Only the last one is your fault, and only the last one is a compliment.

---

### "We rolled out. Adoption spiked then dropped."

Textbook drift. [Habit Loop and Behavioural Drift](/behavioural-change/habit-loop-and-behavioural-drift/) → [Drift Check — 30 60 90](/behavioural-change/drift-check-30-60-90/) → [Embedding and Reinforcement](/behavioural-change/embedding-and-reinforcement/).

The spike was novelty and supervision. The drop is the true baseline. Design for the drop.

---

### "How do we know if it's working?"

[Measurement Plan — Leading and Lagging](/behavioural-change/measurement-plan-leading-and-lagging/). If your only metric is "training completed" you are measuring attendance, not change.

---

### "We need to change the culture."

You can't change culture directly. You change **behaviours**; culture is the residue. Go to [Target Behaviour Definition](/behavioural-change/target-behaviour-definition/) and force the aspiration ("more collaborative", "more accountable") into observable actions. If you can't film it, it isn't a behaviour.

---

### "Everyone's exhausted — there's too much change."

Real and legitimate. [Change Readiness Assessment](/behavioural-change/change-readiness-assessment/) has a saturation section. The honest advice to a senior leader is often *sequence, or stop something*. Bring them the list of what's already running.
