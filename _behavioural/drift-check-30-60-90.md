📅 **Put these three dates in the diary before go-live.** Not after. After go-live, nobody books them, and drift happens in silence.

---

## Day 30 — is it happening at all?

<div class="bct-callout bct-note" markdown="1">
**Day 30**

**Date:** `_______` **Behaviour metric:** `___%` *(vs baseline `___%`)*
**Counter-measures moving?** `_______________`

**Three questions to five people who do the work:**
1. Are you doing it? Honestly.
2. What makes it hard?
3. What do you do instead when it doesn't work?

**What I'm hearing:** `_______________`
**Workarounds spotted:** `_______________`
**One thing to fix this week:** `_______________`
</div>


**Typical picture:** adoption looks good — often better than target. Don't celebrate. This is novelty plus supervision, and it will fall. Say so in the report, so that the fall doesn't read as failure later.

⭐ **The workaround question is the most valuable one.** Workarounds at day 30 are honest design feedback and are still cheap to fix. By day 90 they're established practice and cost ten times as much to unwind.

---

## Day 60 — the real number appears

<div class="bct-callout bct-note" markdown="1">
**Day 60**

**Date:** `_______` **Behaviour metric:** `___%` **Change since day 30:** `___`

**Is the old route still available anywhere?** `_______________`
**Are managers still asking about it?** `_______________`
**Is anyone senior still asking about it?** `_______________`
**Have champions gone quiet?** `_______________`
**What's the excuse being used when it's not done?** `_______________`
</div>


**If adoption has dropped**, work down this list in order — the causes are almost always here:

| Check | If yes |
|---|---|
| Programme support withdrawn? | Nothing replaced it in the line — [Embedding and Reinforcement](/behavioural-change/embedding-and-reinforcement/) |
| Old route still open? | Close it. Most common single cause. |
| Managers stopped asking? | Managers' own attention has moved. Prompt them. |
| It fails under pressure? | Effort accumulation. The design isn't good enough on the worst day. |
| A new initiative landed? | Saturation. Take it to the sponsor. |
| Recognition stopped? | Reward extinction. Move it into the BAU rhythm. |
{:class="table table-single table-narrow"}

⚠️ **Day 60 is the intervention point.** The drop is visible and the behaviour hasn't yet been abandoned. Day 90 is too late for a cheap fix.

## Day 90 — has it become normal?

<div class="bct-callout bct-note" markdown="1">
**Day 90**

**Date:** `_______` **Behaviour metric:** `___%` **Trend:** `___`
**Outcome metric moving?** `_______________`

**The normalisation tests:**
- Do people refer to the old way in the **past tense**? ☐
- Do **new joiners** learn only the new way? ☐
- Does it survive a **busy week**? ☐
- Do people **teach each other** without being asked? ☐
- Is it in **objectives, onboarding and the standing agenda**? ☐
- Would it continue if the change team **vanished tomorrow**? ☐

**Boxes ticked:** `___/6`
</div>


| Score | Read |
|---|---|
| 5–6 | Embedded. Hand over formally and stop. |
| 3–4 | Partially embedded. Fix the unticked boxes — they're specific and actionable. |
| 0–2 | Not embedded. It will revert. Decide honestly whether to re-invest or stop pretending. |
{:class="table table-single table-narrow"}

---

## The honest conversation at day 90

If it hasn't embedded, someone has to say so. It should be you, and it should be framed as a decision rather than a confession:

> "Adoption is at 31% and flat. It's not going to embed on its own. Realistically there are three options: re-invest for another eight weeks with `[specific asks]`; reduce the scope to the part that has stuck and formally drop the rest; or stop and be clear that we've stopped. What I'd advise against is leaving it as-is — a half-adopted process is worse than either the old way or the new one, because now there are two truths."

⭐ That last sentence is the one that gets a decision. Leaders will tolerate slow progress indefinitely; they will act on "we currently have two versions of the truth."

---

## Running drift checks as standard

Once you've done this three times you'll want it as a habit. Suggested standing calendar for any change:

| Date | Check | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Go-live + 30 | Adoption + workarounds | |
| Go-live + 60 | Drift causes | |
| Go-live + 90 | Normalisation tests + [Change Retrospective](/behavioural-change/change-retrospective/) | |
| Go-live + 180 | Single metric check, 15 min | |
{:class="table table-single table-narrow"}

The 180-day check takes fifteen minutes and catches the slow reversion that nobody else is looking for.
